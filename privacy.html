Pixel Waitress Studio: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Pixel Waitress Studio is a private tool that uploads episodes of our pixel-art series Pixel Waitress to our own TikTok account as drafts.

What we access

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's display name and ID, used only to confirm that we are uploading to the correct account.
  • Video upload (video.upload): used only to send our own episodes to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What we store

The sign-in token is stored only on the creator's own computer, so the tool can upload the next episode. We keep a list of the episodes that have been uploaded. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. We do not access other users' accounts, followers, messages or viewing data.

Deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token removes everything the app stored.
