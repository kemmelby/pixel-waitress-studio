Pixel Waitress Studio: Terms of Service

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Pixel Waitress Studio ("the app") is a private publishing tool. Its only purpose is to upload episodes of Pixel Waitress, our original pixel-art drama series, to our own TikTok account as drafts.

Only the creators of Pixel Waitress use the app. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up.
